Privacy Policy — LOHPA Electrical Grimoire

Last updated: August 1, 2026

LOHPA Electrical Grimoire is a field reference and job-logging tool built for electricians. We designed it to stay out of your way and out of your data.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • No user accounts or login
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising identifiers
  • No location data (permission removed)
  • No personal information is sent to our servers

On-Device Media & Job Data

Photos, voice notes, job logs, and calculations you create remain on your device. They leave the device only when you deliberately export, share, or email them.

Permissions

Camera and microphone are requested only when you choose to capture media for a job. Storage is used solely for the files you create.

Purchases & Third Parties

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play, Apple, and RevenueCat. We never receive or store your payment details.

Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If our practices change we will update this page.
