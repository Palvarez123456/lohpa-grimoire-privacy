Privacy Policy — LOHPA Electrical Grimoire

Effective: August 29, 2026  ·  Last updated: August 29, 2026

LOHPA Electrical Grimoire (“the App”) is a field reference and job-logging tool for electricians. It is operated by Paul Alvarez, doing business as LOHPA Electrical Grimoire, in Washington, United States (“we,” “us”).

Design rule: job photos, voice notes, logs, and calculations stay on your device unless you export them. The only data that leaves the device in normal use is purchase/subscription data handled by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat so paid features can unlock.

1. What stays on your device

The following are created and stored locally on your phone or tablet:

  • Job logs, notes, and calculations
  • Photos and images you capture or attach to a job
  • Voice notes and other audio you record for a job
  • App settings and preferences

This data does not go to our servers. It leaves the device only if you choose to export, share, back up, or email it using your own device tools.

If you delete the App, on-device job data is removed with it unless you previously exported it or your operating system independently backed up the App’s local files.

2. What leaves the device

We do not run our own user-account or analytics servers for the App.

If you buy or restore a one-time purchase or subscription, purchase information is processed by:

  • Apple (App Store / StoreKit) on iOS
  • Google Play Billing on Android
  • RevenueCat, which we use to validate receipts and unlock paid features

We do not receive or store your credit card, bank, or full payment details. Apple and Google process payment. RevenueCat receives purchase history and an app user identifier so the App can restore entitlements (Pro / Life and other paid unlocks) across devices and reinstalls.

RevenueCat’s own policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Depending on platform, this off-device purchase data may include:

  • Purchase history and entitlement status
  • An app user ID generated for purchase restoration (anonymous unless you later provide identifying information)
  • Store locale / currency needed to process the purchase

This collection is required to sell and restore paid features. It cannot be turned off if you make a purchase.

3. Permissions

  • Camera — only when you choose to take a job photo.
  • Microphone — only when you choose to record a voice note.
  • Photo library / storage — only to save or attach files you create.

We do not request location. Location permission has been removed.

4. What we do not collect

  • No user accounts or in-app login
  • No advertising identifiers used for ads
  • No advertising, remarketing, or cross-app tracking
  • No analytics SDKs, no crash-reporting SDKs, no advertising SDKs in the current build described by this policy
  • No precise or coarse location
  • No contacts, calendar, or health data

If a future build adds analytics, crash reporting, accounts, cloud sync, or an assistant that sends content off-device, this policy will be updated before that build ships, and the in-app Legal links will point to the new version.

5. Children’s privacy

The App is a professional tool for adult tradespeople. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child provided information to us, email the contact below and we will delete what we can control.

6. Your choices and deletion

  • On-device data: delete jobs, photos, and voice notes in the App, or uninstall the App.
  • Purchases: manage, cancel, or request refunds through your Apple or Google account. We cannot cancel a store subscription from our side.
  • RevenueCat purchase records: email sparkyp123456@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my purchase data” and any app user ID or purchase email you have. We will delete the RevenueCat customer record we control. Store records held by Apple or Google remain under their policies.

7. U.S. privacy requests

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

If a U.S. state privacy law gives you rights of access, correction, or deletion for personal information we control, email the contact below. We will verify the request as reasonably possible and respond. Because the App has no user accounts, most job content never leaves your device and is not in our possession.

8. International users

The App is operated from the United States. Purchase processors may handle data in other countries under their own terms. If you use the App from outside the U.S., you understand that purchase-related data may be processed in the U.S.

9. Changes

If our practices change, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date. Material changes that add new off-device collection will be published before the corresponding App version is released.
